# Iron Man (2008) {#iron-man}

## Overview

**Iron Man** is a *superhero film* directed by Jon Favreau and released in 2008. It kicked off the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) and made ==Robert Downey Jr.== a household name as Tony Stark.

---

## Plot Summary

> "I am Iron Man."
> — Tony Stark

Billionaire weapons manufacturer Tony Stark is captured by terrorists in Afghanistan. Instead of building them a missile, he builds a armored suit to escape. After returning home, he refines the suit and becomes a hero.

### Key Story Beats

1. Tony is captured by the Ten Rings and builds the Mark I in a cave.
2. He escapes and announces that his company will stop making weapons.
3. He builds the Mark II and Mark III suits.
4. He discovers that Obadiah Stane is the real villain.
5. Tony defeats Stane's Iron Monger suit in a final battle.
